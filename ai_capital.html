🔥 Structural Anatomy of the Circular Economy

The 3 Engines of the AI Ouroboros & Systemic Bubble Vectors

How capital flows in a closed circuit between Big Tech, specialized cloud builders, and foundation labs—and what triggers a potential correction.

Loop 1: Captive Cloud Credit Swaps

The Equity-for-Compute Flywheel

Hyperscalers (Microsoft, Amazon, Google) inject billions into frontier labs (OpenAI, Anthropic). Crucially, 70–80% of these rounds are delivered as non-cash cloud compute credits. The startup consumes data centers, which the hyperscaler records as cloud revenue, boosting its share price and justifying expanded Capex outlays to NVIDIA.

Loop 2: Vendor Financing & Debt Collateral

NVIDIA's Neocloud Subsidies

NVIDIA invests equity into Neoclouds like CoreWeave and grants them priority hardware allocations. Neoclouds leverage these guaranteed chips as physical collateral to borrow billions in private debt (Blackstone, Magnetar) and immediately recycle the capital to purchase thousands of additional NVIDIA GPUs.

Loop 3: The Sequoia $600B Question

The Capex vs. Real-Revenue Deficit

To justify data center infrastructure investments exceeding $250B+ annually at historical 50% tech margins, the end-market AI application ecosystem must generate $600B+ in annual software revenue. As of 2026, total commercial GenAI revenue stands at ~$42B, leaving a staggering $270B+ deficit.
